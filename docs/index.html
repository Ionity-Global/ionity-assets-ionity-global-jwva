About

Ionity Assets (ionity-assets-ionity-global-jwva) is the single source of truth for every digital asset belonging to the Ionity Global ecosystem — versioned, discoverable and ready for integration across all Ionity products.

Maintained by Johan Wilhelm van Antwerp (JWVA) under Antwerp Ecosystems Designs Ionity ÆĐï.
